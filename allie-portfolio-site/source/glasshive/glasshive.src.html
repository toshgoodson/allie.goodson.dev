GlassHive
The go-to designer on a CRM/PRM, from scheduling to storefronts.
RoleProduct designer
TeamProduct, engineering, partner devs
FocusCalendar, integrations, marketplace, design ops
ToolsFigma, Adobe XD, Jira

GlassHive is a CRM/PRM that helps tech companies and their partners create, send, and track sales content from one place. I owned core product UX: I rebuilt the calendar into a real scheduling hub, led design on a high-stakes partner integration, shaped the marketplace's vendor storefronts, and moved the whole team from XD to Figma.

01

Calendar redesign

The old calendar was a static grid, so reps booked meetings somewhere else. I mapped every scheduling scenario end to end, then rebuilt it with daily and weekly views, booking links, and automated confirmation emails.
